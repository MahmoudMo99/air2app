Every stroke starts with a simple gesture. Here's how Air2App turns hand movement into shapes you can create, control, and bring to life.

Experience it yourself
AIR2APP / CREATIVE PIPELINE
INPUT CREATION
  1. 01 DETECTION
    CAMERA / INPUT LANDMARKS / TRACKED
    STEP ONE

    We follow your hand.

    Your camera provides the view. MediaPipe detects your hand's landmarks and follows its movement.

  2. 02 CREATION
    GESTURE / PINCH STROKE / CAPTURED
    STEP TWO

    Movement becomes art.

    Pinch your fingers to start drawing. Move through the air to create a digital stroke, then release to finish.

  3. 03 RECOGNITION
    STROKE / ANALYSIS SHAPE / IDENTIFIED
    STEP THREE

    Your idea takes shape.

    Air2App analyzes the finished stroke and checks for circles, squares, or rectangles, while also supporting freehand drawing.

  4. 04 INTERACTION
    OBJECT / SELECTED MOVE / SCALE / ROTATE
    STEP FOUR

    Bring it to life.

    Switch to Interact to move, resize, or rotate your creations. Enable physics to give your scene an extra dimension of motion.
